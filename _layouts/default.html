Email–GitHub–Scholar–LinkedIn

I'm a 3rd-year PhD candidate at Cornell CS, where I'm fortunate to be advised by Prof. John Thickstun on machine learning and generative models.

I'm interning at NVIDIA Fundamental GenAI Research. I interned at MBZUAI Institute of Foundation Models working with Hector Liu and Subham Sahoo (Summer 2024).

I have a BA in Mathematics and a BA in Statistics from Carleton College. During those years, I was fortunate to work with Seth Cooper on generative models, Anna Rafferty on bandits, Christopher Amato on deep RL, and Adam Loy and Claire Kelling on MCMC algorithms and Gaussian Processes.

I received the CRA Outstanding Undergraduate Researcher Award.

Research

I specialize in developing principled, controllable and efficient generative models:

  • Pre-training and scaling: Sigma (NVIDIA Tech Report), RePlaid (NeurIPS 2026), Scaling Beyond (ICML 2026)
  • Transformer architecture design: EC-DLM (COLM 2026), Eso-LMs (ICML 2026), Block Diffusion (ICLR 2025 Oral)
  • Classic problems in RL: Adversarial Bandits (EDM 2022), HILMO (WAFR 2022), RSAC (NeurIPS Deep RL Workshop 2021)
